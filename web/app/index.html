One screen, edge to edge. Maps, music, calls and an assistant, laid out like a head unit and lined up with the fold.

See it on the road.

Sixty seconds. Real screens from the app.

Everything for the drive.
Nothing you don't need.

Maps

Maps

Knows the way. And the stops.

Turn-by-turn with a clear turn banner and rerouting. Add a stop for fuel or coffee, and if the app closes mid-trip it picks up where you were.

  • Your choice of maps: Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze.
  • Saved places with your own names, one tap away.

Music

Your music, front and centre.

Apple Music and Spotify, with big artwork and controls you can hit without looking twice. Playlists and albums list every song; tap any one to play from there.

Calls & Messages

Your people, one tap away.

Call favourites from your contacts. Reply to texts by voice, or with a quick reply like "On my way, ETA 5:42" while you're navigating.

Assistant

Just say where.

"Take me to the airport, but stop for petrol on the way." The assistant finds the stop that adds the least time, then starts the trip.

And there's more.

Your whole drive, on one screen.

Weather

The sky, at a glance.

Hourly and 10-day forecasts, and the weather where you're going before you get there.

Calendar

Next stop: your next meeting.

Day, Month and Upcoming views. Tap Go to drive to any event's place.

Dashcam

Already pointing at the road.

Unfolded on the dash, the rear cameras face forward. Loop recording keeps the last few minutes; tap Save clip to keep the moment.

Car data

Talk to your car.

Plug in a Bluetooth OBD-II adapter for live speed, RPM, temperatures, battery voltage and check-engine codes in plain English.

Home, as you arrive.

HomeKit garage doors, lights and scenes as big tiles, and "Open garage?" as you pull up. Locks are never opened for you.

YouTube for passengers.

Videos for the person in the passenger seat. Never for the driver.

Opens with your car.

A Shortcuts automation opens Duo Dash when your phone joins the car's Bluetooth. Settings walks you through it.

Folded, or on any iPhone and iPad

Fits whatever screen it's on.

On the cover screen, the dock moves to the bottom. On a spare iPhone or an iPad mini, the layout adapts to the window it's given.

On-device speech

Your voice stays on the phone.

Dictation runs on Whisper and replies are spoken by natural Kokoro voices, both on the device. Nothing you say is sent away to be transcribed.

Private by design.

Your places, contacts, settings and trips stay on your iPhone. No sign-up, no analytics, nothing to sell.

  • No account
  • No tracking
  • Data not collected

Why Duo Dash

It started with an older car.

A 2006 sedan with a dash worth keeping, and no screen at all.

Cheap head units weren't the answer.

Slow to start. Short on memory. Thin on sound. And every one of them tore out the factory look of the dash.

Then one device replaced three.

The iPhone Duo took over from a phone and an iPad mini. One thing to carry. One thing to charge. And a screen for the car that leaves with you.

All it needed was the simple part.

Big buttons. A clean layout. Nothing to fiddle with. A screen that looks like it belongs, not a tablet bolted to the console.

So, Duo Dash.
