The problem

Magnetic mounts are made for phones.

A normal MagSafe mount expects the phone's weight to sit right over the magnet.

Then you unfold it

And half of it hangs in the air.

Open an iPhone Duo and the weight shifts to one side. Over every bump it creeps round, until the dashboard is on a tilt.

The fix

A foot under the open half.

The Duo Dash mount's cradle carries both halves, so the screen stays level, bump after bump.

How it works

Three parts.
One steady screen.

Designed around the open phone, not adapted from a mount for a closed one.

Sealed under a 0.6 mm skin

Magnetic cradle

An N52 magnet ring is printed inside the cradle face, so there's no glue line and nothing to peel off. It lines up with the magnets already in the iPhone Duo, or a magnetic case.

17 mm ball · wing-nut collar

Ball joint

Tilt and turn the screen towards you, then lock it with a hand-tight collar. 17 mm is the common size, so many standard bases fit too.

The foot takes the weight

Anti-sag cradle

A shaped foot under the open half holds the phone level over bumps. Twist it a quarter turn and a stop holds it in portrait. Folded, it works like any magnetic mount.

Renders from the CAD model, not photos. The final product may differ in colour and finish.

Two bases in the box

Vent or windscreen.
Swap in seconds.

Loosen the collar, pull the ball out, snap in the other base. Both use the same 17 mm ball.

Vent clip

Low, close and out of the way.

A hook goes behind a horizontal louvre and a knob tightens it, so it doesn't slide off like spring clips do.

  • Keeps the screen below your line of sight.
  • Best for round or wide horizontal vents. Very thin or vertical louvres may not suit it.
  • Heater air can reach 60 °C. Point the vent away from the phone in winter.

Telescopic suction arm

Higher and nearer.

A sticky gel cup with a lever, for the windscreen or a smooth dash. The arm reaches 77–115 mm.

  • For cars whose vents sit low.
  • Fix it low in a corner, outside the wiper sweep and clear of airbag covers.
  • On a hot day, take the phone with you: a windscreen in sun gets far hotter than the cabin.

Better together

Made for Duo Dash.

The free app turns the unfolded Duo into a dashboard: maps, music, calls and an assistant, with big touch targets and a layout that lines up with the fold. A head unit for the price of a mount.

The mount works with any app, and Duo Dash works with any mount. You don't need one to use the other.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Is it legal to use my phone on a mount while driving?

In Australia a fully licensed driver may use a phone for navigation, music and hands-free calls only if it sits in a commercially made holder fixed to the car, and it mustn't block your view of the road. You still mustn't text, browse or watch video. Learner and provisional drivers often can't use a phone at all, even in a holder. Rules differ by state and country, so check your local road authority. Set your route before you drive. The Duo Dash app itself is for passenger use only.

Will it get in the way of an airbag?

It can if you put it in the wrong place. Never mount it on or in front of an airbag cover (the steering wheel hub, the passenger-side dash top, or pillars marked SRS). The vent clip on a centre vent is usually the safest spot. Check your car's manual for where its airbags deploy.

What about heat in summer?

The first batches are printed in PETG, which starts to soften at about 75 °C. A dash in direct summer sun can get hotter than that. On the vent it's fine with the air on cold. On the windscreen, take the phone with you and don't leave the mount in full sun on a 35 °C day. A summer heat test is part of our road testing, and we'll publish the result. A later moulded version will use a more heat-resistant plastic.

Are the magnets safe for my phone, cards and pacemaker?

The magnet ring is similar to the ones in magnetic phone cases and chargers, and doesn't harm the phone. Keep the mount and phone at least 15 cm from pacemakers and implanted defibrillators, the same distance phone makers give for their own magnetic accessories. Don't keep bank cards, hotel keys or parking tickets between the phone and the mount, as the magnet can wipe them. The ring is fully sealed in the cradle and can't come out, but this isn't a toy: keep it away from small children.

Does it work with a case?

Yes, with a magnetic case up to about 2.1 mm thick on the back. A case without magnets will weaken the hold a lot. Very thick rugged cases may not fit the foot.

Will it fit my car?

Most cars have either a horizontal vent or a smooth patch of windscreen or dash that suits one of the two bases. Thin vertical louvres and heavily textured dashes are the usual problems. If it doesn't suit your car, send it back within 30 days for a refund.

Does it work with other phones?

The cradle is shaped for iPhone Duo. Any phone with magnets on the back will stick to it, but the foot and stop are sized for the Duo, so other phones may not sit level.

Does it charge?

No, there's no charger inside. A side clip keeps a USB-C cable tidy so you can plug in as usual.

When will it ship?

After road testing (bumps, rough roads, summer heat) is done. We'll email everyone on the list with a ship date before taking any payment. Each batch is printed and checked by hand, so the first batches are small.

Returns and warranty?

30 days to return it for any reason, and a 12-month warranty. These are on top of your rights under Australian Consumer Law, which can't be excluded: if it has a major failure you're entitled to a replacement or refund.
